# How to read a write back

A read back is the person's one chance to catch a mistake before it reaches the CRM. Show every
field that will be saved, in plain words, in the same order as the proposal.

## Shape

```
Here is what I will save to the CRM. Nothing is saved yet.

Note: <summary>
- Contact: <name> (<company>)
- Property: <property>            (leave out if none)
- Deal: <deal> (<stage>)          (leave out if none)
- When it happened: "<their words>" (kept as your words, no date set)
- Discussed: <item>; <item>
- Next steps: <item>; <item>

Task: <title>, due <date or "no date given">

Flags: <each flag from the proposal, or "none">

Reply yes to save it, or tell me what to change.
```

## Rules

- Include every flag the proposal returned. Never hide one.
- Show the person's words for times, in quotes. If the CRM set a date, show it too.
- Do not show internal ids unless the person asks; names are enough to check.
- One read back per proposal. If they change something, propose again and read back again.
- If the proposal was refused (a duplicate, a missing record), say what the CRM said and what you
  need from them. Do not try a different change on your own.
