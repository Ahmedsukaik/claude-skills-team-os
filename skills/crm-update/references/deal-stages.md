# Deal stages

Use these names exactly. A deal moves forward one stage at a time as a rule, but the CRM accepts
any stage the broker asks for; read the move back clearly when it skips stages.

| Stage | Means | The broker might say |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | a prospect, no tour yet | "new lead", "just reached out" |
| Touring | at least one tour booked or done | "we toured", "showing it next week" |
| Proposal sent | written proposal or term sheet sent | "sent the proposal", "sent terms" |
| LOI signed | letter of intent signed by the client | "LOI signed", "they signed the LOI" |
| Lease negotiation | lease drafts going back and forth | "in lease negotiation", "redlines" |
| Closed won | lease signed | "signed the lease", "closed it", "won" |
| Closed lost | the client chose elsewhere or stopped | "lost it", "they went elsewhere", "dead" |

If the broker's words are not in the right column, or fit two stages ("they're basically done"),
ask which stage they mean.
