# Meeting brief template

```
<meeting title>, <when the calendar says>, <length>
With: <people> (<company>)
Property: <property, if any>

Where it stands
- Deal: <deal name>, <stage>, last activity <date> (<N> days ago)
- Last note (<date recorded>): <summary>; <one or two facts>

Open items
- <open task, with its due date or "no date">
- <a concern from the notes, in the client's words>

Ask about
- <a question that closes one of the open items>
- <a question that closes another>
```

Rules: only facts from the calendar and the CRM; "nothing in the CRM" where a section is empty;
no more than three "Ask about" lines.
