# Email style for client follow ups

## Shape

```
Subject: <what it is about, under 8 words>

Hi <first name>,

<One line that ties to the last conversation: "Thanks for walking 310 Canal Street with me.">

<What you are sending or confirming, from the notes and open tasks.>

<The next step, with a date only if the notes or the broker gave one, in their words.>

Best,
<broker's first name>
```

## Voice

- Warm, short and specific. Plain words, no jargon the client did not use.
- One idea per paragraph. No more than four paragraphs.
- No exclamation marks in a row, no emojis, no "I hope this email finds you well".
- Never attach or promise a document the notes do not mention.

## Example

```
Subject: HVAC specs for 310 Canal Street

Hi Marcus,

Thanks for walking 310 Canal Street with me.

You mentioned the freezers and the HVAC capacity. I'm pulling the HVAC specs for the building
and will send them over, along with times for a second tour with your CFO.

Best,
Priya
```
